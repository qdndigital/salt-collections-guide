This page covers the account and system controls under **Settings**, **Profile** and **Audit Log**.

## Profile settings

Open the **account menu → Profile settings** to manage your own account:

- Name and contact details.
- **Language** (English / Vietnamese) — saved to your account.
- **Password** — change it here.
- **Two-factor authentication** — see [Signing in](/docs/getting-started/signing-in).

## System settings

Admins configure organization-wide behaviour under **Settings**, including:

- **Two-factor** policy.
- **Approval thresholds** for expenses and payouts.
- Connection and defaults used across the app.

## Audit log

Every meaningful change in SaltCollections is recorded. **Audit Log** shows *who* did *what*, *when* — the user, the affected record, and the before/after values.

![The Audit Log](/assets/sc-audit-logs.png)

Use it to:

- Investigate an unexpected change.
- Confirm who approved a payout or edited an expense.
- Satisfy compliance and internal-control requirements.

<Callout type="note" title="Records are retained, not erased">
Important records aren't deleted outright — instead they carry a **status** (e.g. an expense is *rejected* or *voided*) and can be **locked**, so the history stays intact. Combined with the audit log, you always have a full trail.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="Security hygiene">
Enable 2FA for every admin and accountant, keep approval thresholds sensible, and review the audit log periodically. These three habits prevent most finance-system incidents.
</Callout>
