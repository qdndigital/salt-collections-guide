The **Owner Portal** is a separate, read-only space for third-party **property owners**. They sign in and see only *their* properties — transparent performance and money, with no access to your operations.

## Getting an owner in

1. Open the owner's record under **Owners**.
2. Select **Send Owner Portal invite** to email them an invitation.
3. They set a password and sign in to the portal.

<Callout type="note">
Owners see a completely different, restricted view — never your admin screens, other owners' data, or internal operations.
</Callout>

## What owners see

- **Dashboard** — an overview of their properties for the selected period, with a transparency statement.
- **Properties** — each property on a single page with stacked sections: overview & balance, bookings, expenses and contract.
- **Reports** — their own **P&L** and **Occupancy**, matching what you see internally for their units.
- **Payouts** — payout history and status, so they know exactly what they've been paid and what's outstanding.
- **Documents** — statements and files you share with them, available to download.
- **Settings** — their profile and language.

## Sharing documents

To give an owner a statement or contract PDF, add it to their **Documents** from the admin side — it appears in their portal to download. This replaces emailing files back and forth.

<Callout type="tip" title="Transparency builds trust">
The portal shows owners the same numbers you use internally. Keeping bookings synced and expenses tagged means owners self-serve accurate reports — fewer statement requests for your team.
</Callout>
