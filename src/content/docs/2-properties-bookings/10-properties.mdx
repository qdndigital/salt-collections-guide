Everything in SaltCollections hangs off your **property structure**. Setting it up correctly once means revenue, expenses and reports all attribute themselves to the right place.

## The structure

```
Company            legal entity that owns/operates
  └─ Building       physical address (a block of rooms)
      └─ Property   a rentable unit (villa, apartment, room set)
          └─ Room   individual bookable room
```

You don't have to use every level — a single villa can be one property with one room. Larger operators map buildings with many properties and rooms.

## Companies

Under **Companies**, add each legal entity you operate. Companies own contracts and appear on invoices and reports. Create one with **Add company**.

## Buildings

**Buildings** hold the physical address and group properties that sit at one location. Useful for shared costs (electricity, cleaning) that you later split across the units inside.

## Properties & rooms

![The Properties list](/assets/sc-properties.png)

Under **Properties**, select **Add property** and set its name, type, building and owner. Open a property to:

- **Edit** its details.
- Manage its **Rooms** — add, edit, and set per-room attributes used by P&L-by-room.

## Owners

Third-party **Owners** are the people whose properties you manage. Under **Owners → New owner**, capture their contact details. From an owner's record you can:

- See their properties and balances.
- Select **Send Owner Portal invite** to give them self-service access — see the [Owner Portal](/docs/owner-portal/overview).

## Contracts

A **Contract** defines the money arrangement between an owner and a property — how much of the revenue they get, and when they're paid.

1. Go to **Contracts → New contract**.
2. Pick the **owner** and **property**, and give it a **contract number**.
3. Choose the **contract type** — *Revenue share*, *Profit share*, *Fixed rent* or *Hybrid* — and set the terms. The fields shown depend on the type:
   - **Owner share (%)** — the revenue/profit split.
   - **Fixed amount (VND/month)** — a flat monthly figure.
   - **Minimum guaranteed** — a floor the owner is promised per period.
4. Set the **start/end dates**, **payout frequency** and **payout day of month**.
5. Add **notes** and optionally attach the signed **contract file (PDF)**.

<Callout type="tip" title="Contracts drive payouts">
The contract terms feed **owner balances** and **payouts** automatically. Get the share percentage and payout schedule right and the finance side largely runs itself.
</Callout>

## Channels, categories & vendors

- **Channels** — your distribution sources (Airbnb, Booking.com, direct…). Bookings carry a channel so the dashboard can show revenue mix.
- **Categories & Vendors** — expense categories and the suppliers you buy from, reused across every expense.
