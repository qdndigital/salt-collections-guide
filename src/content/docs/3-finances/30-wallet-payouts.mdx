The **Wallet & Balance** area is where the money actually moves: your cash and bank accounts, reconciliation against statements, and the balances you owe property owners.

## Wallet accounts

Under **Wallet → Accounts**, set up each place money sits. Accounts can be a **Cash**, **Bank**, **E-wallet**, **Payment gateway**, **Credit card** or **Other** type. Create one with **New account**; open an account to see its running balance and transactions.

![Wallet accounts with balances](/assets/sc-wallet.png)

### Reconciliation

Match SaltCollections' record against your real bank/cash statement:

1. Open the account and select **Reconcile**.
2. Pick the period and tick off the transactions that appear on your statement.
3. Compare the **expected closing balance** with the **statement balance** and resolve any difference before finishing.

Reconciling regularly is what keeps the wallet trustworthy.

### Cash flow

**Wallet → Cash flow** shows money in vs money out over time across accounts — your liquidity picture at a glance.

## Owner balances

**Owner balances** is what you owe each owner, derived from their **[contract](/docs/properties-bookings/properties)** terms against actual revenue and expenses. You don't add these by hand — use the **Compute period** action to build a balance for a period. A balance moves through *Draft → Finalized → Partially paid → Paid out*. Only a **Finalized** balance with an outstanding amount can be paid.

## Owner payouts

![Owner payouts list with statuses](/assets/sc-payouts.png)

When a balance is finalized and outstanding, pay the owner:

1. Go to **Payouts** and select **Create payout** (or start from an owner balance).
2. Choose the **owner balance** and the **account to pay from**.
3. Enter an **amount** — leave blank to pay the full outstanding balance.
4. Set the **scheduled date**, add notes, and submit.

<Callout type="note" title="Owner needs bank details">
A payout can only target an owner who has **bank details** on file. If the balance option is greyed out as "missing bank", add the owner's bank account first.
</Callout>

### The payout lifecycle

A payout is deliberately multi-step so money doesn't leave without sign-off:

1. **Propose** — you create it (status *Proposed*).
2. **Approve** — an authorized user approves it (*Approved*).
3. **Mark processing** — the transfer is being made (*Processing*).
4. **Mark paid** — confirm it landed. This requires a **transfer reference** (and optionally a proof attachment). → *Paid*.

From *Processing* you can instead **Mark failed** if the transfer bounces. You can **Cancel** at any point before it's paid.

<Callout type="tip" title="Approval thresholds">
Large payouts and expenses can require sign-off. Admins set the limits under **Settings → Approval thresholds**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="note">
Owners can watch their own balances and payout history in the [Owner Portal](/docs/owner-portal/overview) — no need to email statements around.
</Callout>
