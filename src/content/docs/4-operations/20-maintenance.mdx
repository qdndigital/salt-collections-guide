The **Maintenance** area keeps properties in good shape — both the things that break and the upkeep you plan ahead.

![The Maintenance area](/assets/sc-maintenance.png)

## Maintenance requests

When something needs fixing, go to **Maintenance → New** and capture:

- **Title** and **description** of the issue.
- The **property** it's at. (A room/building selector just narrows the asset picker — it isn't stored on the request.)
- Optionally the **asset unit(s)** affected, and up to **5 photos**.
- A **priority**.

You **assign** and **track status** afterwards from the request's own page — they aren't set on the create form.

### Assigning and closing out

Open a request to move it through its workflow. **Assign** hands it to a **vendor** (the supplier who'll do the work). When work is done and billed, attach the cost — SaltCollections creates a matching **expense** (source *Maintenance*, status *Pending approval*) against the property, so maintenance spend flows straight into your [P&L](/docs/finances/reports).

## Preventive schedules

**Maintenance → Schedules** covers recurring upkeep — servicing air-con, testing alarms, deep cleans. Create a schedule with a **property**, **title**, **description**, **frequency** and **next due date**, and SaltCollections generates the due requests automatically so nothing slips.

<Callout type="note">
Upcoming and overdue maintenance can trigger [notifications](/docs/operations/notifications) so the right person is reminded in time.
</Callout>
