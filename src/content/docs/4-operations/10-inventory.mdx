The **Inventory** area tracks the physical things your properties run on — consumables, minibar stock and fixed assets — with stock levels, movements and low-stock alerts per property.

![The Inventory overview](/assets/sc-inventory.png)

## Items & stock

- **Items** — your catalog of consumables, minibar products and asset items, organized by category.
- **Stock** — current quantity of each item per property.
- **Locations** — where stock is held.

## Moving stock

Every change in quantity is a recorded **movement**, so you always know why a number changed:

- **Stock in** — receive stock. SaltCollections updates the **weighted-average cost** automatically.
- **Stock out** — consume or discard stock.
- **Transfer** — move stock between properties/locations.
- **Movements** — the full history over time.

## Minibar

**Minibar** tracks in-room consumables tied to bookings, so guest consumption flows into the right room and gets charged/costed correctly.

## Cycle counts

Keep records honest with periodic counts:

1. Go to **Cycle count → New**.
2. Count the physical stock for the selected items/location.
3. Enter the counts — SaltCollections shows variances against the system quantity.
4. Confirm to adjust.

## Purchase orders

Under **Purchase orders → New**, raise a PO to a vendor, then receive it into stock when it arrives (which becomes a stock-in movement).

## Assets & valuation

Beyond consumables, SaltCollections handles **assets**:

- **Asset catalog** and **assets** — furniture, appliances, equipment per property.
- **Depreciable** — assets that amortize over time, tracked by status (*In stock*, *In use*, *Fully amortized*, *Written off*) with current book value and a **report damage** action.
- **Valuation** — total worth by category: stock value + depreciable-asset value + fixed-asset value.
- **ABC analysis** — a Pareto ranking that tags items **A / B / C** by consumption value, so you focus on what matters.

## Alerts

**Stock alerts** flags items that are out or near their minimum threshold, so you reorder before you run dry. These also surface in [Notifications](/docs/operations/notifications).

<Callout type="tip" title="Set thresholds">
Alerts only fire if items have a **default minimum stock** (and, ideally, a **default reorder quantity**) set. Configure these when you create an item so the reorder scan has something to compare against.
</Callout>
