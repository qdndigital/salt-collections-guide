You reach SaltCollections through your organization's web address. An administrator creates your account and sends an invite — you don't sign up yourself.

![The SaltCollections sign-in screen](/assets/sc-login.png)

## Sign in

1. Open the SaltCollections URL your admin gave you.
2. Enter your **email** and **password**.
3. Tick **Remember me** on a trusted device to stay signed in longer.
4. Select **Sign in**.

If two-factor authentication is enabled on your account, you'll then be asked for a **6-digit code** from your authenticator app.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)

2FA adds a second step at login using an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password, etc.).

1. Go to **Settings → Two-factor**.
2. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app — or type the **manual secret key** shown next to it.
3. Enter the 6-digit code to confirm and enable it.

<Callout type="warning" title="Don't lose your authenticator">
There are no printed backup codes. If you lose access to your authenticator app, an administrator has to reset 2FA on your account before you can sign in again.
</Callout>

## Changing your password

While signed in, change your password anytime under **account menu → Profile settings → Password**: enter your **current** password and a **new** one.

## Forgot your password

<Callout type="warning" title="Email reset isn't live yet">
The **Forgot password?** screen exists, but the reset **email is not sent yet** — so you won't receive a link. Don't rely on it to get back in. Instead, ask an administrator to reset your password (below).
</Callout>

If you're **locked out**, contact an administrator. They open your account under **Users**, choose **Reset password**, and the system generates a **temporary password shown once** — they pass it to you, then you sign in and change it under **Profile settings**.

## Signing out

Open the **account menu** (top-right / bottom of the sidebar) and select **Sign out**. Always sign out on shared computers.
