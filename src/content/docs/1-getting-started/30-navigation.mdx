After signing in you land on the **Dashboard** — a snapshot of how your properties are performing this month.

![The SaltCollections dashboard: KPI cards, revenue trend, revenue mix and the sidebar](/assets/sc-dashboard.png)

## The dashboard

The dashboard greets you by name and summarizes the selected period across all properties you can see:

- **KPI cards** — **Revenue**, **Expenses**, **Gross profit** and **Occupancy rate**, each with margin, RevPAR and ADR at a glance.
- **Revenue & profit by day** — a 30-day trend chart.
- **Revenue mix** — a breakdown by distribution channel.
- **Top 5 properties by profit** — ranked, with revenue, profit, margin and occupancy.
- **Action needed** — alerts that want your attention: overdue payments, check-ins within 24 hours, unusual utility spikes and more.

Use the **period picker** (top of the page) to change the date range — presets include *Last 7 days*, *Last 30 days*, *Last 90 days*, *Month*, *Quarter*, *Year*, *Year to date*, or a *Custom* range. It defaults to the current month.

<Callout type="note">
Fresh install? The dashboard fills in once GoHost sync brings in bookings and you start entering expenses. Empty cards just mean there's no data yet.
</Callout>

## The sidebar

The left sidebar is split into section headers. What you see depends on your role and permissions:

- **Operations** — the everyday menu: Dashboard, Bookings, Expenses, Reports, Wallet & Balance, Inventory, Maintenance.
- **Owner access** — Owner Portal (only shown if your account also has the owner role).
- **Administration** — grouped into:
  - **Properties & Entities** — Properties, Buildings, Companies, Channels, Owners.
  - **GoHost Sync** — the integration.
  - **System Admin** — Users, Roles, Audit Log, Notifications, Settings.

Use **Open menu / Close menu** to collapse the sidebar on smaller screens.

## Switching language

SaltCollections speaks **English** and **Vietnamese**. Open the **account menu → Profile settings** and change your language — it's saved to your account and applies everywhere you sign in.

## Getting around a record

Most detail screens — a booking, an owner, a property — are a **single scrolling page** built from stacked cards: a summary header up top, then sections for the detail (guest info, finances, activity, notes). Scroll down to see everything; there's usually no tabbed navigation to hunt through.
