Welcome 👋 **SaltCollections** (shown in the app as **The Salt Collections**) is the finance and operations command centre for rental-property businesses — hotels, homestays, villas and serviced apartments. It syncs bookings and revenue from your **GoHost** PMS, then adds the financial control, inventory, notifications and owner reporting that the PMS alone doesn't cover.

## What it does

- **Pulls bookings & revenue** from GoHost automatically, and keeps them in sync.
- **Tracks every expense** — per property, per room, with VAT and vendor detail.
- **Reports profit** — P&L by property, room or owner, plus occupancy, ADR and RevPAR.
- **Manages cash** — wallet accounts, reconciliation, owner balances and payouts.
- **Runs operations** — inventory, minibar, maintenance and stock alerts.
- **Keeps owners informed** — a self-service Owner Portal with transparent reports.

## Who this is for

SaltCollections serves several roles, and what you see depends on yours:

- **Admin / CEO / CFO** — full view: reports, decisions, configuration.
- **Manager** — day-to-day management of assigned properties.
- **Accountant** — expenses, wallet, reconciliation, payouts.
- **Operations staff** — bookings, inventory, maintenance data entry.
- **Property owner** — read-only Owner Portal for their own properties.

## A note on language

SaltCollections works in **English and Vietnamese**. Your language is saved per user — switch it any time from your **Profile settings**. Amounts are shown in Vietnamese đồng (VND).
